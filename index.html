Frequently asked questions

Is SnapLink free to use? +

This front-end demo uses a third-party service that currently offers a no-key shortening endpoint. Availability and terms may change; check the provider's documentation before production use.

How do I make a QR code? +

After a link is created, SnapLink generates a QR image for that short URL. Scan it with a phone camera or open the QR image to save it.

Will this website be approved for Google AdSense? +

No template can guarantee approval. Before applying, publish useful original content, working About / Contact / Privacy Policy / Terms pages, secure your site, and review Google's current publisher policies. Ads must never be disguised as buttons or placed to encourage accidental clicks.
