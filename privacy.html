Privacy Policy

This draft policy describes the current static SnapLink demo. Review and update it to match your hosting, analytics, and legal requirements before publishing.

Information you submit

When you request a short link, the URL is sent to os8.me. Avoid entering sensitive, private, or confidential URLs.

Third-party services

The website loads styling/fonts from third-party CDNs and sends link-shortening requests to os8.me. Those providers may process technical data under their own policies.

QR codes

The QR code is generated locally in your browser from the returned short URL. The QR library is loaded from a third-party CDN.

Logs and hosting

Your web host may collect server logs such as IP address, user agent, and request time. The exact data depends on your hosting provider.

Your choices

Do not use this service for confidential links. Contact the site owner to ask a privacy question. Update this policy if analytics, advertising, accounts, or cookies are added.
